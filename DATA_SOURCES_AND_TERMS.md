## City of Tacoma Open Data

Zoning, building footprints, Accela permits, steep slopes, wetlands, biodiversity areas, protected-water buffers, streams, and the City-hosted flood layer are sourced from City of Tacoma open-data services.

The City describes Tacoma Open Data as having no legal restrictions on use or reuse. Source: [Tacoma Open Data & Maps](https://tacoma.gov/services/tacoma-open-data-maps/).

Attribution: **City of Tacoma**. Data are dynamic and supplied for public information. This independent project is not produced, reviewed, or endorsed by the City.
